    About me

    Hi, I’m Aleah.

    I’m a Bachelor of Multimedia Arts graduate with experience in graphic design, social media, website content, and virtual assistance.

    I create visual content such as social media graphics, branding materials, marketing assets, and website designs while keeping everything consistent with a brand’s look and direction. I also assist with social media posting, content organization, basic SEO analysis, research, and other day-to-day creative and administrative tasks.

    I enjoy combining creativity with organization—helping brands maintain a professional online presence while making sure projects, content, and everyday tasks stay on track.
